# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize e-commerce sales performance.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of key business metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Year-on-Year Growth
- Category-wise Profit
- Category-wise Sales Share
- State-wise Sales
- Top Subcategories by Sales
- Regional Performance
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Year-wise Performance

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights that can help understand business performance and identify important sales and profitability trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
2. Track sales and profit trends over time.
3. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
4. Analyze profitability across product categories.
5. Identify top-performing product subcategories.
6. Analyze sales performance across different states.
7. Compare sales performance across regions.
8. Understand customer segment performance.
9. Analyze year-wise sales performance.
10. Present business insights using an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales and Profit Analysis

The Sales and Profit Analysis section shows the monthly performance of:

- Sales
- Profit

This helps identify changes in sales and profitability throughout the year.

---

### 2. Category-wise Profit

The dashboard compares profit generated by major product categories:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

The visualization makes it easier to compare the contribution of each category to overall profit.

---

### 3. Category-wise Sales Share

A donut chart is used to visualize the percentage contribution of each category to total sales.

The displayed sales shares are:

| Category | Sales Share |
|---|---:|
| Furniture | 32.30% |
| Office Supplies | 31.30% |
| Technology | 36.40% |

---

### 4. Sales by State

A geographical map is used to visualize sales performance across different U.S. states.

The map allows users to identify areas with relatively higher and lower sales performance.

---

### 6. Region Analysis

The dashboard provides filters for the following regions:

- Central

### 7. Customer Segment Analysis
